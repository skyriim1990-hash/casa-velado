## Evening

Someone stays in the _galera_ tonight. The first one was a barn of timber and corrugated iron. The leaves hang in their rows, as they have for weeks, drying and changing colour. The doors are open or shut according to the air, and the air is what we are here to watch.

## Night

The air turns damp, or it turns dry, and the doors change with it. There is little else to do. This is the quietest part of the work, and the part everything else depends on. The house is named after it: _velar_ is Spanish for to keep watch.

## Morning

The watch ends. What was seen goes into the ledger. That is how we learned to work: the houses that bought our leaf in the first years told us which of it had been cured too fast, and we wrote it all down.
