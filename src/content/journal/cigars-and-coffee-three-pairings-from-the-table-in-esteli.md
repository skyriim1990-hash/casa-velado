Three pairings, each the one we give on the cigar’s own page.

## With a short cigar

[Jalapa Petit Corona](/cigars/jalapa-petit-corona): _a milky coffee, early in the day._ It is the shortest cigar we make, 4½ inches and about half an hour.

## With a morning cigar

[Jalapa Corona](/cigars/jalapa-corona): _black coffee, or tea without milk._ A 5½ × 42 that takes about forty-five minutes.

## With the cigar we smoke at home

[Galera Robusto](/cigars/galera-robusto): _an afternoon espresso, or a dark rum without ice._ It is the cigar we smoke most often at home, medium-bodied, even, about an hour.
