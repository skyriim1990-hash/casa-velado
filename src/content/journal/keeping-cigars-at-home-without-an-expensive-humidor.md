A humidor is one way to keep cigars. It is not the only way, and it is not what a cigar needs first. Here is a short list.

1. **Keep them in cedar.** Our cigars are aged in cedar rooms, and the boxes of the Cosecha 2020 and the Muestrario are cedar.
2. **Read the box.** The harvest year and the months of ageing tell you how long the leaf has already waited.
