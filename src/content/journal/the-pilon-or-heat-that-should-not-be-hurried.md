A _pilón_ is a stack of leaves left to ferment, turned at intervals so the heat stays even. The heat is the point of it, and the danger.

In our first years we sold our leaf to other houses, and they told us which of it had overheated in the pilón. Those conversations are part of why we write everything down.

Fermentation is not hurried. Every line has its own time, set down in the ledger: four months for Jalapa, six for Galera, seven for La Vela and eight for Cosecha 2020. The Pilón line has nine months and is fermented twice, and it is named for this stage.

How often the stack is turned, and at what temperature, are figures we have yet to confirm, so we do not print them here.
