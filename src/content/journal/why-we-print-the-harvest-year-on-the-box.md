On every box we print the harvest year and how many months the leaf has aged. It is not a fashion. It is how the ledger has been kept on the farm since the first day.

The year is a plain fact. Our lines come from the harvests of 2020, 2021 and 2022, and a box says which one. Beside it is the number of months the leaf has aged, which is different for every line: from 18 months for Jalapa to 60 for Cosecha 2020.

> We print nothing on the box that is not in the ledger.

We began by selling our leaf to other houses, and they taught us to write everything down: which leaf had ripened, which had been cured too fast, which had overheated in the pilón. A box is where we show the same figures to the person who smokes the cigar.
