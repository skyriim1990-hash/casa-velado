## What it is

_Entubado_ is a rolling method in which each filler leaf is folded into a small tube, for an even draw. The filler is the _tripa_. The tubes are held together by the binder, the _capote_, and the cigar is finished with the wrapper, the _capa_.

## Why it is the slower way

Each leaf is handled on its own, folded and laid with the others. The fold is for the draw: an even one, from the first third to the last.

## By hand

Every cigar we make is rolled by hand. We do not roll by machine.
